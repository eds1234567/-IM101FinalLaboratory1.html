# Mission 3 Reflection

## 1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?

Among AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform, AWS impressed me the most because of its wide range of cloud services and global infrastructure. I learned that AWS provides services for computing, storage, networking, databases, security, and many other workloads. Its variety of services makes it useful for different types of organizations and applications.

## 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?

AWS, Azure, and GCP are all major public cloud platforms that provide on-demand computing resources through the internet. They all offer similar categories of services, including virtual machines, object storage, databases, networking, identity management, and Kubernetes. They also allow organizations to scale their resources depending on their business and technical requirements.

## 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?

Business requirements are important when selecting a cloud provider because organizations have different needs, budgets, technologies, and workloads. For example, a company using Microsoft products may benefit more from Azure, while an organization focused on artificial intelligence and machine learning may consider GCP. The best cloud platform is therefore not always the most popular one, but the one that best fits the organization's requirements.

## 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud platform requires analysis rather than simply choosing a provider based on popularity. I learned how to compare cloud services, identify the strengths of different providers, and match cloud solutions to specific client scenarios. This activity helped me understand how technical decisions can support business goals.
